# Seasonal wind sprites

Four original sprites generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen on 2026-09-14:
spring petal, summer grass blade, autumn leaf and winter snow dust.
Exact prompts: `tools/wind-generation.json`. Local masters: `art-source/wind/`.
Technical alpha cleanup, cropping and compact WebP export: `tools/prepare-wind.py`.
No third-party stock images. These particles are separate from the stationary terrain.
