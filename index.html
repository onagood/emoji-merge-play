HOW TO PLAY
👇
Tap to drop. It falls where you tap.
Two of the same touch → they merge into the next one!
Land a third between two of a kind → all three merge and skip a step. Double points!
🔥
Merge again right away to keep a combo going. Each one multiplies your score.
🎉
Two of the biggest piece vanish together for a 1000-point jackpot.
Don't pile past the red line, or it's game over. A shake can save you once per round.
🔓
Make a set's last piece to unlock the next emoji set.
🌱
Points raise your level, and every round pays coins. Spend them in the shop to fill your meadow.
